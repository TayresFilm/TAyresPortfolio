About

I'm a freelance videographer with a particular pull toward sports — motorsports, horse racing, and fencing among them. Being close to live competition means learning to read a moment before it happens, so the footage carries the same energy the crowd feels.

Alongside sports work, I produce content for social platforms, from quick-turn short-form videos to full live-streamed productions, always built around what each client needs to say and who they're saying it to.

My time with Doswell Operating Group and St. David's Anglican Church shaped how I work today — both gave me room to sharpen the technical and creative sides of the job, and I'm grateful for the mentorship that came with it.
